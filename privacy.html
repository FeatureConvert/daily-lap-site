Daily Lap Privacy Policy

Daily Lap, by Nexrax · Last updated October 5, 2026

Short version: Daily Lap needs no account and never asks who you are. If "Share my laps" is on, your best lap times and lap replays go to our server under a made-up name, so other players can race your ghost. Nothing else is collected. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

On your phone

Your best laps, medals, settings and the cars you own are saved on your phone. They don't leave it unless "Share my laps" is on, as described below.

What goes online with "Share my laps" on

"Share my laps" is on to start with, and the game says so after your first best lap, with a switch to turn it off. While it's on, the game sends our server:

  • A random player id and a secret code, made on your phone the first time one of your laps is shared. The server keeps the id and a scrambled (hashed) copy of the code, so only your phone can send laps as you.
  • A made-up name made from the id, such as "Swift Otter 42". Nobody types a name.
  • Your best lap on each track you race: the time, the sector times, the car, and the lap replay (where the car went and how you steered), so it can appear on the boards and as a ghost for other players.
  • Dates: when your first lap was shared, and when each lap was set.

What we never collect

Your real name, email, contacts, location, photos, Game Center or Apple Account details, device identifiers or the advertising identifier. The server runs on Cloudflare; like any website it sees your internet address while it answers, but we don't store it, and we keep no request logs.

With sharing off

Turning "Share my laps" off (in Settings, or on the finish card) stops all sending. The game still downloads the boards and other players' ghosts, without sending anything about you.

How long it's kept

Your shared laps are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until Daily Lap's online boards close. Turning sharing off stops new laps from being sent; it doesn't remove laps already shared.

Removing your laps

Email Nexrax@icloud.com with your made-up name (it's shown in Settings, under "Share my laps"). We'll remove your player id, name and every lap you've shared.

Who sees it

Other players see your made-up name, lap times and ghost on the boards. We don't sell or share data with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game only learns which item you bought so it can give it to you, and remembers that on your phone.

Children

Daily Lap is made for everyone. It never asks for personal information, so it collects none from children. The made-up names and lap replays can't identify anyone, and sharing can be switched off in Settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
