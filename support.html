Common questions

I bought something but don't have it.

Tap the track's name at the top of the race screen to open the menu, tap the gear for Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. The Archive Pass, the Emergency Pack and the Toy Box Pack come back on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Restore Purchases is also in the Garage and on every purchase card.

How do the online boards and ghosts work?

With "Share my laps" on, your best lap on each track goes up under a made-up name (like "Swift Otter 42"). The board shows where you stand, and the ghost of the lap just ahead of yours races you. Only your lap times and lap replays are shared, nothing else. See the privacy policy.

How do I turn sharing off?

Open Settings (the gear in the menu) and switch Share my laps to Off. Nothing is sent after that, and you can still race everyone else's ghosts. To have laps you've already shared removed, email us your made-up name.

When does the new track start?

Every day at 09:00 UTC. Everyone in the world gets the same track.

Why is my lap not on the board?

Laps go up when you're online and sharing is on; if you were offline, the game sends your best lap next time it can. Every track has its own board, and the race screen shows where you stand on it.

Are there ads?

No. Never. No energy, no timers and no loot boxes either.

Privacy

Daily Lap shares only your lap times and lap replays, under a made-up name, and only while "Share my laps" is on. See the privacy policy.
