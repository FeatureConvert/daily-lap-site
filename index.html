Daily Lap

Daily Lap · one new race track every day, for iPhone. No ads, ever.

Support · Privacy policy

Every day there's a new race track, the same for everyone in the world. One lap, about 40 seconds, as many tries as you like.

Your car drives itself. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer, and hold a little longer to drift. Your best lap drives beside you as a see-through ghost, and the clock tells you, sector by sector, whether you're ahead of it or behind.

Today's track is free, every day, and so are twenty classic tracks and three cars. A few optional one-time purchases add the past daily tracks and some extra cars for fun. Every car drives exactly the same, so nobody can buy a faster lap.

No ads, no tracking, no energy, no timers, no loot boxes.
