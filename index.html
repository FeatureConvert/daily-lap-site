Daily Lap

Daily Lap · one new race track every day, for iPhone and iPad. No ads, ever.

Support · Privacy policy

Every day there's a new race track, the same for everyone in the world. One lap, about 40 seconds, as many tries as you like.

Your car drives itself. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer, and hold a little longer to drift. Your best lap drives beside you as a see-through ghost, and the clock tells you, sector by sector, whether you're ahead of it or behind.

Give a friend your friend code, or type theirs in: friends show on today's board, and you can race their ghosts. Watch any lap back, slow or fast, with its racing line drawn on the road. No accounts: the boards use made-up names.

Today's track is free, every day, and so are twenty classic tracks and fifteen cars. A few optional one-time purchases add the past daily tracks and some extra cars for fun. Every car drives exactly the same, so nobody can buy a faster lap.

No ads, no tracking, no energy, no timers, no loot boxes.
